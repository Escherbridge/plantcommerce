# Conductor program records

`tracks.md` is the active, evidence-based program index. A track is `planned`
until its implementation and verification evidence are recorded; a historical
plan must never be marked complete merely because it describes code that exists.

The 2026-07-21 marketplace program replaces four incompatible visual tracks.
Their source directories remain intact as archived records, while
`archive/superseded-20260721.md` records the retained requirements and the
replacement tracks that own them. New or edited Conductor Markdown must have
OKF frontmatter with a `type` field.

The catalog, checkout, identity, and affiliate capability gates are release
contracts. They may be specified in Conductor, but neither the plan nor its UI
may imply that they are production-enabled before their database rehearsals and
operational evidence are approved.
