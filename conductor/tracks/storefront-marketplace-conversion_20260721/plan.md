# Delivery plan

1. Freeze the public product, media, cart, checkout, and affiliate route/event
   contracts.
2. Remove silent mock fallbacks and preserve the truthful public hold until its
   catalog and schema prerequisites are proven.
3. Select and verify the canonical checkout flow; remove/deprecate the unsafe
   competing path only with database-baseline evidence.
4. Model the four Grow Plans and expose them only after their required catalog,
   cart, checkout, and attribution gates pass.
5. Verify the complete browser journey during the integrated release gate.
