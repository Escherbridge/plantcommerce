# Source capture: W3C WCAG 2.1 — Reflow

- URL: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/reflow.html
- Fetched: 2026-07-22 (America/Denver)
- Page update: 2026-06-12
- Intent: define responsive behavior for 320px catalogue, filters, PDP, cart, and checkout surfaces.

## Captured source facts

- Success Criterion 1.4.10 is Level AA.
- Vertically scrolling content must work at a width equivalent to 320 CSS pixels without loss of information/function or two-dimensional scrolling, except content whose meaning truly requires two dimensions.
- W3C notes that 320 CSS pixels corresponds to a 1280px-wide viewport at 400% zoom.
- Sticky/fixed content can harm reflow and obscure focused content.

## Aevani implication

Use a single-column fallback at 320px, wrap toolbars and chips, avoid fixed card widths, and confirm sticky headers/cart bars do not consume the usable viewport or hide focused controls.
