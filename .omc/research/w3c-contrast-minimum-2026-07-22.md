# Source capture: W3C WCAG 2.1 — Contrast (Minimum)

- URL: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/contrast-minimum
- Fetched: 2026-07-22 (America/Denver)
- Page update: 2026-06-01
- Intent: define accessible text contrast constraints for a lime/chartreuse accent direction.

## Captured source facts

- Success Criterion 1.4.3 is Level AA.
- Normal text requires at least 4.5:1 contrast against its background.
- Large-scale text requires at least 3:1.
- Logos and inactive controls are exceptions in the normative criterion.
- W3C emphasizes luminance contrast rather than hue alone; saturated color does not guarantee legibility.

## Aevani implication

Use chartreuse as an accent surface, border, focus support, badge, or decorative signal only when its foreground/background pair is measured. Default body and control text should remain a high-contrast navy/near-black unless a tested inverse pairing passes.
