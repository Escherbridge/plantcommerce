# Source capture: W3C WCAG 2.1 — Focus Visible

- URL: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/focus-visible
- Fetched: 2026-07-22 (America/Denver)
- Page update: 2025-09-17
- Intent: define the minimum keyboard focus behavior for catalogue, cart, and checkout controls.

## Captured source facts

- Success Criterion 2.4.7 is Level AA.
- Every keyboard-operable interface needs a mode with a visible focus indicator.
- The indicator must remain visible while focus remains; it cannot be time-limited.
- W3C lists browser-default focus, author-supplied indicators, two-color indicators, and `:focus-visible` as sufficient technique patterns.

## Aevani implication

Use a consistent, persistent `:focus-visible` treatment that survives both warm neutral and dark navy surfaces. A two-layer indicator is safer than a single chartreuse outline across heterogeneous cards and imagery.
